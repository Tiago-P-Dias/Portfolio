CarRace

Made in Pyhton

CarRace Demonstration

CarRace is a Python-based 2D racing simulation designed to model real-time vehicle movement and speed dynamics around a closed circuit. The application features a custom track layout, an automated race car, and an active HUD displaying live telemetry—tracking elapsed time (t) and instantaneous velocity (V) as the car navigates sharp turns, accelerates down straightaways, and completes laps across the finish line
